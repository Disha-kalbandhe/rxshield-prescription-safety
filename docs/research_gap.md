# Research gap

Prior work includes prescription OCR, handwritten prescription recognition,
medication safety systems, drug interaction detection, and ML-based medication
error detection. Open challenges include fragmented systems, limited labeled
prescription-error data, privacy restrictions, explainability, generalization,
and connecting image understanding with medication-safety reasoning.

RxShield does not claim to be the first system or to provide clinical
validation. This document records a research direction for later milestones.
